## Why

The Screen Effects form is clipped by the focused editor shell and differs from the familiar Alerts presentation. Operators need reachable controls and a consistent editing workflow.

## What Changes

- Fit the editor to its viewport with variant navigation, a central silent canvas and a scrolling tabbed inspector.
- Align module inventory and collapsible browser-source presentation with Alerts.
- Preserve saved documents, live confirmations and explicit save/test behavior.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `screen-effects`: Require viewport-safe editing and consistent module presentation.

## Impact

Screen Effects React components, CSS, Storybook, focused tests and browser workflow coverage. No API, persistence or playback changes.
